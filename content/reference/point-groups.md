[[commands/csom|`csom -p/--pg`]] accepts any of these 47 point groups in Schoenflies notation. All of them are analyzed when the flag is omitted.

```text
C2   C2h  C2v  C3   C3h  C3v  C4   C4h  C4v  C5   C5h  C5v
C6   C6h  C6v  C7   C8   Ci   Cs   D2   D2d  D2h  D3   D3d
D3h  D4   D4d  D4h  D5   D5d  D5h  D6   D6h  D7h  D8h  E
I    Ih   O    Oh   S10  S4   S6   S8   T    Td   Th
```

> [!warning]
> Names are case-sensitive and must match exactly: `Oh`, not `oh` or `OH`.
